# LIFE: Every Choice Has a Cost

A single-player life simulation game where every decision has consequences.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Enter your name and country.
2. Start with 100 AED.
3. Make choices throughout your life.
4. Manage your money, health, academics, reputation, and relationships.
5. Complete 30 days across two acts.
6. See your final report card at the end.

## 🕹️ Features

- 30-day life simulation
- School life and adult life
- Money and expenses
- Random events
- Relationships
- Jobs
- Travel
- Health and reputation
- Final report card
